# Strategic Approach to AI Development in the Enterprise

## Executive Summary

The most successful enterprises do not treat AI as a technology initiative.

They treat AI as a **business transformation capability** supported by:

* Governance
* Platforms
* Data
* Security
* Operating Models
* Change Management

A common mistake is:

```text id="2rj9z0"
AI Strategy = Build Chatbots
```

A more mature perspective is:

```text id="z6ql5f"
AI Strategy = Build Enterprise Capability
```

The objective is not to deploy a few AI applications.

The objective is to create a repeatable system that enables hundreds of AI use cases to be developed, governed, secured, and scaled across the organization.

# Final Strategic Principle

The most successful enterprises do not scale AI by building more AI applications.

They scale AI by building an enterprise capability consisting of:

```text id="x7a17l"
Business Strategy
      +
Governance
      +
Platform Engineering
      +
Data Foundation
      +
Security
      +
Operating Model
```

The long-term goal is not to create individual AI solutions, but to establish a secure, governed, reusable AI ecosystem that enables every business unit to build and operate AI solutions at enterprise scale.
